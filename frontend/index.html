一活動宗旨

緣起於「丙午年青年策劃人員藏脩息遊班」，以「以交流促進友誼、以參與凝聚青年、以實踐推動惠民啟蘊」為理念，結合運動聯誼、文化美食市集及交流參訪，促進臺澳青年相互認識、橫向學習與共同實踐。

二活動資訊

活動期間
2027年07月14日（三）至22日（四），共9日（含往返及息遊）
主要活動
07月17日（六）臺澳青年運動賽事聯誼
07月18日（日）文化與美食小型市集
活動地點
澳洲昆士蘭州布里斯本中心、黃金海岸區域；市集實際場地待確認。
參與對象
第一階段以 18 至 30 歲之大專學生及社會青年為主，招募範圍為臺灣臺中區與澳洲各州青年。
招募規模
總人數暫定上限 150 人（含隨行人員），依場地及接待條件調整。
辦理單位
主辦：澳洲昆士蘭州布里斯本
協辦：南澳、西澳及北領地等地區

三活動內容

  1. 07/14（三）臺灣出發（依航班確認）
  2. 07/15（四）抵達布里斯本、環境熟悉與交流
  3. 07/16（五）活動準備、分組、場勘及流程確認
  4. 07/17（六）運動賽事聯誼、黃金海岸沙灘排球
  5. 07/18（日）文化與美食小型市集：文化分享、美食體驗與青年服務
  6. 07/19（一）～07/21（三）息遊、參訪、交流及返程準備
  7. 07/22（四）返抵臺灣（以實際航班為準）

說明：

  • 運動聯誼以交流為主、競賽為輔，擬評估氣球排球、躲避飛盤、樂樂棒球、足壘球、桌球及趣味競賽等項目；實際項目依籌備結果公布。
  • 市集以臺澳共同規劃攤位及文化美食體驗為原則。

四參與方式及青年服務

青年依興趣參與運動聯誼、文化美食市集與相關籌備服務。鼓勵臺澳青年共同規劃、分工與執行；實際工作分配、隊伍編排及行前培訓，由籌備團隊另行通知。

五費用及交通住宿

本活動採自主籌辦方式，經費來源及分擔方式，由參與者與各地區籌備團隊協調確認；實際金額以報價及核定結果為準。住宿規劃以布里斯本中心為主，實際安排依接待條件調整。

1Purpose

This program grows out of the Bingwu-year (2026) Youth Planners “Cangxiu Xiyou” Class. Guided by the ideals of building friendship through exchange, uniting youth through participation, and promoting community benefit and inspiration through action, it combines a sports day, a cultural & food market and exchange visits, so that young people from Taiwan and Australia can get to know each other, learn from one another and work together.

2Event details

Dates
Wed 14 – Thu 22 July 2027, 9 days (including travel and leisure days)
Main events
Sat 17 JulyTaiwan–Australia youth sports day
Sun 18 JulySmall cultural & food market
Venue
Brisbane CBD and the Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia. Market venue to be confirmed.
Who can join
Phase one focuses on university students and young adults aged 18–30, from Taichung (Taiwan) and all Australian states.
Capacity
Up to 150 people (including accompanying staff), subject to venue and hosting capacity.
Organisers
Host: Brisbane, Queensland
Co-hosts: South Australia, Western Australia, Northern Territory and other regions

3Program

  1. Wed 14 JulDepart Taiwan (subject to flights)
  2. Thu 15 JulArrive in Brisbane, settle in and get to know each other
  3. Fri 16 JulPreparation, team allocation, site visits and run-through
  4. Sat 17 JulSports day & Gold Coast beach volleyball
  5. Sun 18 JulCultural & food market: cultural sharing, food tasting and youth service
  6. Mon 19 – Wed 21 JulLeisure, visits, exchange and preparing to return
  7. Thu 22 JulReturn to Taiwan (subject to actual flights)

Notes:

  • The sports day is about friendship first, competition second. Activities under consideration include balloon volleyball, dodge frisbee, T-ball, kickball, table tennis and fun games; the final list will be announced.
  • Market stalls and cultural / food experiences will be planned jointly by Taiwanese and Australian youth.

4How to take part

Join the sports day, the cultural & food market and related preparation work according to your interests. Taiwanese and Australian youth are encouraged to plan, share tasks and deliver the program together. Task allocation, team arrangements and pre-departure training will be announced by the organising team.

5Costs, transport & accommodation

This program is self-organised. Funding and cost-sharing will be agreed between participants and each region's organising team; final amounts depend on quotes and approval. Accommodation will mainly be in Brisbane CBD, subject to hosting arrangements.
